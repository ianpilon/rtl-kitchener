Trailer Refrigeration Units: Makes & Models

The current makes and models of semi-trailer reefer units in North America, focused on the two makers that own the market.

Bottom line: Over-the-road trailer reefers in North America are effectively a two-maker market: Carrier Transicold and Thermo King together hold nearly the entire installed base. Between them they currently list about 15 trailer unit models. This page covers trailer units only. Self-contained truck and van units (Supra, V-series, T-series) are a separate category.

Scope: North America only, trailer units only, models the manufacturers list as current on their North American product pages.

2. The three drive architectures

Trailer reefers split into three power designs.

ArchitectureHow it worksModels
Diesel direct-driveDiesel engine drives the compressor mechanically (belts)Carrier X4 7500 / 7700; Thermo King Precedent C and S diesel units
Diesel-electric hybridDiesel engine drives a generator; refrigeration runs on electricity; electric standby when plugged inCarrier Vector family (E-Drive); Thermo King Precedent S-750i
All-electric / enginelessNo onboard engine; battery or shore power onlyNot yet a mainstream NA trailer line (Thermo King Advancer-e is European)
On the hybrid: Carrier's hybrid is the Vector family, using E-Drive (diesel engine to generator, all-electric refrigeration, electric standby on shore power). All four Vector single-temp and multi-temp units use this architecture. Thermo King's hybrid-electric equivalent is the Precedent S-750i. The Carrier X4 and the diesel Precedent units are conventional diesel direct-drive, not hybrid.

4. Service manual availability online

Whether each of the 15 current models has a service manual findable online for free. Carrier publishes one combined Operation & Service Manual per unit (the full repair manual), while Thermo King splits a separate Maintenance Manual (the service manual) from its operator and installation manuals.

Verdict: 14 of 15 have a service or maintenance manual findable online for free. The lone gap is the Thermo King Precedent S-750i, where only the operator and installation manuals are public; its full service manual appears dealer-restricted, consistent with it being the newest hybrid unit.

5. Scope notes

  • Trailer units only. Self-contained truck and van units (Carrier Supra; Thermo King V, T, e-series) are excluded.
  • North America only. European and Asian trailer lines differ (for example Thermo King Advancer and SLXi, Carrier Vector HE).
  • Current models. Recently retired generations are not listed.
  • Intermodal and rail reefer units are a separate adjacent category and are not counted here.
